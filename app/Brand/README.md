# Baton identity

Ibrahim chose burgundy/black and requested I.A in the compact logo. The signed compact asset is bundled into BatonUI and used for empty states and the running app's Dock icon. Earlier sheets remain as design history.

- `baton-logo-concept-v1.png`: transparent signed wordmark and handoff emblem.
- `baton-logo-review-v1.png`: the concept on silver and graphite backgrounds, with app-icon and monochrome studies.
- `baton-logo-palettes-v2.png`: earlier graphite/silver, plum/copper and coral/ink comparison.
- `baton-logo-burgundy-v3.png`: earlier burgundy/champagne, silver and blush comparison.
- `baton-logo-burgundy-black-v4.png`: the selected palette's signed wordmark and unsigned compact study.
- `baton-icon-burgundy-black-signed.png`: final compact handoff mark with I.A inside the lower-right corner. Its identical runtime copy lives at `Sources/BatonUI/Resources/Brand/baton-icon.png`.

The emblem combines two conversation shapes into a B silhouette, joined by a diagonal baton. The signed wordmark and final compact icon include Ibrahim's `I.A` maker's signature. A light backing keeps black readable on graphite.

Generated with the built-in image-generation tool. Prompts and review limits are in `docs/plan/reports/UI5-2026-10-06.md`. Rendered at 58/92 points for compact/full empty states; the native fixture shows the signature. The existing menu-bar symbol and decision badge are retained because the small signed artwork has not been verified at 16–22-point menu-bar size.

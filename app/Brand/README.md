# Baton identity

Ibrahim chose burgundy/black and requested I.A in the compact logo. The transparent signed mark is bundled into BatonUI and used for empty states, the menu bar and the running app's Dock icon. Earlier sheets remain as design history.

- `baton-logo-concept-v1.png`: transparent signed wordmark and handoff emblem.
- `baton-logo-review-v1.png`: the concept on silver and graphite backgrounds, with app-icon and monochrome studies.
- `baton-logo-palettes-v2.png`: earlier graphite/silver, plum/copper and coral/ink comparison.
- `baton-logo-burgundy-v3.png`: earlier burgundy/champagne, silver and blush comparison.
- `baton-logo-burgundy-black-v4.png`: the selected palette's signed wordmark and unsigned compact study.
- `baton-icon-burgundy-black-signed.png`: earlier white-tile compact study with I.A.
- `baton-mark-transparent.png`: current cutout containing only the B handoff and I.A, with no white tile. Its identical runtime copy lives at `Sources/BatonUI/Resources/Brand/baton-mark.png`.

The emblem combines two conversation shapes into a B silhouette, joined by a diagonal baton. The signed wordmark and final compact icon include Ibrahim's `I.A` maker's signature. Light surfaces show the cutout directly. Dark surfaces add a close-fitting gray glass backing in code so black and I.A remain visible.

Generated with the built-in image-generation tool. Prompts and review limits are in `docs/plan/reports/UI5-2026-10-06.md`. Rendered at 58/92 points for compact/full empty states; the native fixture shows the signature. The menu bar uses the same 22-point signed mark and retains its decision badge. I.A is present but necessarily tiny there; the larger in-app marks carry the readable signature. Appearance follows macOS without changing system settings.

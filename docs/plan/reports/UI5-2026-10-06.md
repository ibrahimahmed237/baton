## Design direction

The mark depicts a handoff between two conversation forms, arranged around a central baton and a compact B silhouette. The palette comes from the existing app: slate `#20242D`, silver `#DFE6EE`, blue `#1763B5`, teal `#007B75`, and graphite `#50545D`. The wordmark is a restrained contemporary sans serif; the small `I.A` signature belongs to the signed lockup, while the icon remains legible at menu-bar size.

The distinctive feature is the handoff geometry. Avoid generic chain links, AI sparkles, circuit boards or a collection of decorative effects. The empty screens will later pair the quiet mark with one concrete explanation and a relevant action, with user-facing sentences supplied through the note catalogue.

### Primary transparent concept

```text

Asset type: first original logo concept for Baton, a macOS app that links a conversation in one coding agent to a conversation in another and passes their history safely between them.

Constraints: genuinely transparent background. Original mark, professional and memorable, the signature readable but small. No mockup devices, no scene, no extra text, no explanatory labels, no tagline.
```

## What changed

Conversation cards identify their sequence as “Turn N”, show the originating tool and time, and pair each tool's identity with its state. Reached notes use the existing engine text and sit inside their reached turn card directly below its messages, attached by a short inset rail. If a filter hides that turn, its note remains above the visible conversation; if the filter leaves no visible turns, the note is suppressed. Linked chat folders use compact path chips with middle truncation, a folder icon and the full path on hover and to accessibility tools. Message content, order, filters, fold controls, jumps, paths and palette values are unchanged.
